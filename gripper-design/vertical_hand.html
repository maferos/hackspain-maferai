Vertical uncap and pipette hand

The forearm comes straight down beside the vial. From its flange hangs a cage 154 mm wide: the iris clamp's housing plus 4 mm to each 4 mm side plate. Inside it the parabolic cradles grip the vial, the iris clamp slides in over the cap, unscrews it and slides straight back with it, the micropipette hanging on the axis above it goes down, and the clamp comes back to screw the cap on.

    Layout

    Cage. A U-channel of two 4 mm side plates and a back plate, 154 mm wide: the iris housing's Ø138 plus 4 mm of clearance to each plate. That clearance is the only width the design spends. Everything hangs inside: the gripper's beam spans between the plates at their foot, the clamp's rail is bolted to the +Y plate, the pipette's rail to the −Y plate, and the servo head under the UR flange closes the top at the back. 232 mm deep, 485 mm from the plates' foot to the flange.

    Gripper. The same parabolic cradles 21 mm under the cap seat, now exactly as wide as themselves: each jaw reaches back to its carriage through tabs in its own comb bands, where the other jaw's wing tip never comes (1 mm, like the comb), so nothing sits outboard of a cradle's back. Fully open on the 100 ml the gripper spans ±70 mm, 2.6 mm from the plates. Its servo rides behind the beam, under the clamp's path (10 mm). parabolic_gripper.html keeps the arm-behind-the-hub mount; the cradles are identical.

    Iris clamp. The same six-blade clamp and planetary train, on a carriage that slides straight back along the +Y plate: 90 mm out its housing stops 4 mm short of the pipette's rings, and its lift column rides over its own housing, so the clamp never grows wider than the housing. The housing is a closed ring, so it travels 30.5 mm up on that column, 9.5 mm over the cap, and lowers onto the cap only once it is centred; the same column follows the cap up the thread and pulls it straight off the neck before the carriage slides back.

    Micropipette. Fixed on the vial's axis, held by two arm plates off a carriage on the −Y plate. Parked with its tip 4 mm above the clamp's motor, it only goes down: 200 mm from the parked height to 20 mm over the floor, once the clamp is out. That is the price of the width: the flange sits at 530 mm, 100 mm higher than a side-parked pipette would need. The plunger has its own actuator on the pipette's frame.
