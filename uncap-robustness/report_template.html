Can the iris clamp open, pipette and close every amber bottle?

What the v2 hand changed

v1 is the shipped rig. v2 raises the gripper's axis 14 mm with L fingertips that keep the pads where they were, lets the iris close to 80° with the same squeeze, and screws the cap back until the torque says it is seated. Same trials, one by one.

Where each bottle breaks

Every trial counted once, by the first stage that did not pass. The stages run in the order of the sequence.

One factor at a time

Design A. Each cell is one level of one factor with all others nominal, run at the three fills (25, 50, 80 %): the number is how many of the three succeeded. Hover a cell for the failure behind it.

Tolerance

The widest run of tested levels around the nominal where all three fills succeed. “none” means the nominal itself fails for that bottle.

Everything at once

Design B. Pitch, yaw, approach and position error drawn together at random for the bottles that clear the table in the nominal pose. Each dot is a trial, coloured by its first failure.

Margins when it works

Successful trials only, all designs. The tick marks the median. How close each success came to its limit.
