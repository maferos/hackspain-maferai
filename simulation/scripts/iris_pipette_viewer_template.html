    Layout

    The jaws grip the bottle body from the side, 34 mm below the shoulder, tool horizontal: the top of the bottle is free and the hand takes the unscrewing torque. The hinge is bolted on the gripper body itself, on the arm's side: two cheeks on the 2F-85 base carry the axle just ahead of it, over the fingers, 90 mm from the bottle axis and level with the iris plane. The cap therefore starts straight up, and while it unscrews (2 turns × 2.5 mm) the hinge only follows it by 3.2°. Swung up, the clamp lies back over the gripper with the cap, 75 mm clear of the pipette. The dive stops before the ejector arm reaches the neck.

    The pipette sits in a swing frame on a vertical pin in the carriage of a slide beside the bottle. It swings at one height, tip 15 mm over the neck: while the clamp works it stands swung 80° out to the arm's side, beside the gripper, 18 mm clear of the clamp at every hinge angle; once the clamp is up it swings in over the neck (16 mm clear of the folded clamp on the way), and only then does the slide take it down. It never has to park above the arc the clamp sweeps (232 mm), so the mast ends 125 mm lower and the rail only runs from the dive to the neck. Locked in, the pipette is held by two rings on the handle, 44 mm apart; the frame turns on one pin in two bearings 80 mm apart, a cone seats it in a receiver on the carriage and a self-locking gearmotor holds it there, so the plunger and the dive cannot move it.

    Every bottle shares the neck and the cap, so the hand is placed on the neck: a taller bottle only reaches further down, a wider one moves the flange back along the pad fit of the 2F-85, with the mount plate slotted on the gripper to stay on the axis.

    The .blend and the simulation files

    This page, the Blender file and the MuJoCo / Isaac Lab models are built from one description, simulation/scripts/iris_pipette_rig.py (layout, body tree, joint couplings) with the sequence in iris_pipette_plan.py, so they cannot drift apart. From simulation/:

    1. python scripts/generate_iris_pipette_viewer.py — this page, ur10e_iris_pipette.html at the repository root. Open a step with #step=N.
    2. python scripts/generate_iris_pipette_scene.py — assets/ur10e_iris_pipette/ur10e_iris_pipette.xml (attachable MJCF), reference_bottle.xml, the meshes, models/iris_pipette_scene.xml and the URDFs for Isaac (models/iris_pipette_rig.urdf, reference_bottle.urdf, reference_cap.urdf). Then python scripts/iris_pipette_play.py --view plays the sequence in MuJoCo and checks it; iris_pipette_isaac.py is the Isaac Lab twin.
    3. uv run --no-project --python 3.11 --with bpy --with mujoco python scripts/generate_iris_pipette_blend.py — assets/ur10e_iris_pipette/ur10e_iris_pipette.blend and .glb: the sliders on the left are custom properties on the hand object (lift_z, grip_aperture, hinge_angle, iris_angle, housing_turns, pipette_tip, swing_angle, plunger), every joint an empty driven from them, the bottle and the cap carried by Child Of constraints, and the 19 steps keyframed at 30 fps.

    In the simulators the bottle and the cap change hands by the rule this page uses (welds in MuJoCo, pose writes in Isaac), not by contact; assets/ur10e_iris_pipette/FULL_PHYSICS_ISAAC.md says how to make the grip, the blades and the thread real in Isaac.
