Each finger is a closed four-bar linkage. Every link angle follows the aperture through a polynomial fitted to MuJoCo's own solution of the 2F-85 (the same fit drives ur10e_hand.blend), so the pads stay parallel and sweep up along an arc as they close. The arm stub is fixed; the wrist roll turns the flange and everything on it.
