## Working definition

Geometric fabrics are bent Finsler geometries used as reactive motion policies, in which velocity-dependent metrics and zero-work bending terms let behaviour be designed in independent parts while remaining provably stable and path consistent.

## Evidence

- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-004]] — Geometric fabrics are defined as bent Finsler geometries, where Finsler systems give velocity-dependent metrics and bending terms enable independent shaping of associated policies.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-016]] — Geometric fabrics are presented as a strict generalization of classical mechanics, with classical systems being one type of fabric.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-020]] — Under equality constraints, a constrained geometric fabric is shown to be itself a geometric fabric with a modified bending term.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-021]] — With a lower-bounded potential and bounded positive-definite damping, a forced geometric fabric converges to a local minimum of the constrained potential minimization problem.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-024]] — The energized system obtained from an HD2 geometry and a Finsler energy is proven to be a geometric fabric, termed an energized fabric.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-040]] — In the particle experiments, geometric fabrics produced more consistent paths across speed levels without any launching artifacts.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-044]] — The authors state that geometric fabrics allow heightened obstacle avoidance behavior without shifting the system minima.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-055]] — Geometric fabrics on average approached the wall about 50% closer than the RMP in the wall barrier experiment.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-057]] — The geometric fabric let the robot pass through all rings to reach its target, whereas the RMP could not pass the last two rings.
- [[Wyk2021geometric - Geometric Fabrics Generalizing Classical Mechanics to#^wyk2021geometric-060]] — The geometric fabric policy yielded a 0.4% collision rate versus 8.18% for the RMP, measured as the percentage of time steps in collision.
