## Working definition

Training object pose estimators on large sets of images rendered from 3D object models, often physically based and physically simulated, in place of or in addition to scarce annotated real images.

## Evidence

- [[Hodan2024bop - BOP Challenge 2023 on Detection, Segmentation and Pose#^hodan2024bop-010]] — For the 2020 challenge the organizers provided 350K physically-based rendered training images produced with BlenderProc2.
- [[Hodan2024bop - BOP Challenge 2023 on Detection, Segmentation and Pose#^hodan2024bop-011]] — The PBR training images helped DNN-based methods catch up with PPF-based methods in the 2020 challenge.
- [[Hodan2024bop - BOP Challenge 2023 on Detection, Segmentation and Pose#^hodan2024bop-044]] — Trained only on synthetic PBR images, the YOLOv8-based detector still achieves 76.9 APC on seen-object detection.
- [[Hodan2024bop - BOP Challenge 2023 on Detection, Segmentation and Pose#^hodan2024bop-017]] — Synthetic training data from MegaPose were used as the unified training dataset for the unseen-object tasks.
- [[Hodan2018bop - BOP Benchmark for 6D Object Pose Estimation#^hodan2018bop-027]] — Synthetic training images were rendered with fixed lighting conditions and a black background from viewpoints sampled on a sphere.
- [[Hodan2018bop - BOP Benchmark for 6D Object Pose Estimation#^hodan2018bop-055]] — Varying lighting conditions present a serious challenge for methods relying on synthetic training RGB images generated with fixed lighting.
- [[Labbe2020cosypose - CosyPose Consistent multi-view multi-object 6D pose#^labbe2020cosypose-018]] — The authors generate one million synthetic training images on each dataset in addition to the real training images provided.
- [[Labbe2020cosypose - CosyPose Consistent multi-view multi-object 6D pose#^labbe2020cosypose-041]] — On YCB-Video, pre-training the model on synthetic data yields an improvement of approximately 2 points on the AUC of ADD(-S).
- [[Labbe2022megapose - MegaPose 6D Pose Estimation of Novel Objects via Render &#^labbe2022megapose-010]] — The synthetic training dataset consists of 2 million photorealistic images depicting over 20K models in physically plausible configurations.
- [[Wen2023foundationpose - FoundationPose Unified 6D Pose Estimation and Tracking of#^wen2023foundationpose-064]] — In total, the synthetic training dataset for pose estimation contains about 600K scenes and 1.2M rendered images.
- [[Wen2023foundationpose - FoundationPose Unified 6D Pose Estimation and Tracking of#^wen2023foundationpose-056]] — The gain from increasing the amount of synthetic training data saturates around 1M, measured by AUC on YCB-Video.
- [[Xiang2017posecnn - PoseCNN A Convolutional Neural Network for 6D Object Pose#^xiang2017posecnn-038]] — For training on both datasets, 80,000 synthetic images were generated by randomly placing objects in a scene.
