## Working definition

A geodesic synergy is a minimum-energy joint coordination given by a geodesic of the robot's configuration-space manifold under the kinetic-energy metric, and combining a few such synergies generates a wide range of physically meaningful motions.

## Evidence

- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-012]] — As shown by Neilson et al., each geodesic constitutes a coherent coordination of joint angles with minimum energy demand, corresponding to a geodesic synergy
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-013]] — Cited human motion studies indicate that human point-to-point motions follow a single geodesic synergy in the configuration space manifold
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-025]] — For two geodesic synergies sharing an initial position, novel motions are obtained by solving the geodesic equation with a weighted sum of initial velocities
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-029]] — The authors note that combinations of geodesic synergies obtained through this procedure are themselves geodesics on the configuration manifold
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-032]] — Geodesic synergies inherit the benefits of classical synergies, as a wide range of motions can be generated by combining few well-selected synergies
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-033]] — Geodesic synergies are specifically designed to account for the nonlinearities arising from the intrinsic dynamics of robotics systems
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-034]] — The authors describe geodesic synergies as physically meaningful and interpretable, contrasting them with PCA-based Euclidean synergies commonly used in robotics
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-035]] — Geodesic synergies directly encode dynamic robot motions, as opposed to postural and kinematic synergies, which disregard the robot dynamics
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-036]] — The minimum-energy demand of geodesic synergy trajectories is described as especially relevant for energy-constrained systems such as prostheses, exoskeletons and mobile robots
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-039]] — The authors contend that geodesic synergies may be extracted from human motions using principal geodesic analysis, the Riemannian equivalent of PCA
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-041]] — Transferring human geodesic synergies to robots generally remains an open problem, although it may be relatively straightforward for anthropomorphic robot designs
- [[Jaquier2022riemannian - Riemannian geometry as a unifying theory for robot motion#^jaquier2022riemannian-046]] — The authors advocate designing Riemannian optimal controllers based on geodesic synergies, which would allow the robot to react appropriately to external events
