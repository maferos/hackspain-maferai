## Working definition

The time evolution of the joint activity of a population of neurons, viewed as trajectories of a possibly input-driven dynamical system in a low-dimensional neural state space.

## Evidence

- [[Sussillo2016lfads - LFADS - Latent Factor Analysis via Dynamical Systems#^sussillo2016lfads-001]] — LFADS is introduced as a method to infer latent dynamics from simultaneously recorded, single-trial, high-dimensional neural spiking data.
- [[Sussillo2016lfads - LFADS - Latent Factor Analysis via Dynamical Systems#^sussillo2016lfads-002]] — LFADS implements the hypothesis that a driven nonlinear dynamical system provides a reasonable model of many neural processes.
- [[Sussillo2016lfads - LFADS - Latent Factor Analysis via Dynamical Systems#^sussillo2016lfads-003]] — The primary goal of LFADS is to infer smooth dynamics from recorded neural spike trains on a single-trial basis.
- [[Sussillo2016lfads - LFADS - Latent Factor Analysis via Dynamical Systems#^sussillo2016lfads-062]] — The authors propose an emissions model for calcium imaging as an extremely useful extension for inferring underlying firing rate dynamics.
- [[Chung2021neural - Neural population geometry An approach for understanding#^chung2021neural-002]] — Manifold-like representations arise when a set of neurons in a biological or artificial network shows variability in response to stimuli or recurrent dynamics.
- [[Chung2021neural - Neural population geometry An approach for understanding#^chung2021neural-037]] — In a closed dynamical system, state-space trajectories cannot cross themselves, since one point cannot have two different rates of change.
- [[Chung2021neural - Neural population geometry An approach for understanding#^chung2021neural-038]] — A tangling index was introduced to identify when trajectories of recorded neural populations actually cross or come close to crossing.
- [[Chung2021neural - Neural population geometry An approach for understanding#^chung2021neural-045]] — Dynamic motifs in recurrent networks have long been related to cognitive functions, such as fixed points to memory and line attractors to integration.
- [[Farshchian2018adversarial - Adversarial Domain Adaptation for Stable Brain-Machine#^farshchian2018adversarial-007]] — Kao et al. used past population dynamics to partially stabilize a BMI under severe loss of recorded neurons.
