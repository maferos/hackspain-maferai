## Scope

Perception that gives a robot arm the poses it acts on: 6D object pose estimation and its benchmarks, equivariant networks and rotation representations for learning from 3D data, and camera-robot calibration and visual servoing that tie image measurements to the arm. It groups the topics whose concepts are perception models, datasets or camera-robot geometry; end-to-end policies sit under Optimization and learning on manifolds.
