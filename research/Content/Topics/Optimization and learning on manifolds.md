## Scope

Methods that compute motions, controls or model parameters while respecting geometric structure: Riemannian and Lie-group optimization and motion policies, information geometry and natural-gradient training, trajectory optimization and model predictive control, and the training and fine-tuning of visuomotor and vision-language-action policies. It groups the topics whose concepts are algorithms; the underlying geometry sits under Geometry of motion and representation, and perception under Computer vision for manipulation.
