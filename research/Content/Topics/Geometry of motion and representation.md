## Scope

The mathematical structures that describe robot motion and learned or neural representations: Lie groups and screw theory for arm kinematics and dynamics, probability distributions, filters and generative models defined on Lie groups, and the geometry of neural manifolds in deep networks and motor cortex. It groups the topics whose concepts are geometric objects or measurements; methods that optimize or learn over these structures sit under Optimization and learning on manifolds, and perception pipelines under Computer vision for manipulation.
